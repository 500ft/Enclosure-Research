# deSouza et al. (2022): Love My Air Calibration Network

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.5194/amt-15-6309-2022  
**Evidence boundary:** Network calibration study using commercial Canary-S boxes. Enclosure material and airflow geometry are not reported.

## Sensors

Plantower PMS5003 optical PM sensor with integrated temperature/RH measurements and cellular communication.

### Pros

- PMS5003 enables low-cost, high-frequency PM2.5 monitoring.
- Integrated meteorological measurements support environmental correction.
- Cellular communication supports a distributed network.
- Multiple co-location sites reveal calibration-transfer limitations.

### Cons

- PM response is affected by humidity, aerosol properties, site, and time.
- A calibration developed at one site or period may not transfer.
- Fan and optical chamber create maintenance and power demands.
- Integrated temperature/RH readings may reflect the box microclimate.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Commercial outdoor Canary-S box; detailed material, inlet, outlet, airflow, and internal layout are not reported.

### Pros

- Commercial integration enabled deployment across many schools.
- Complete packaging and cellular communication supported operational use.

### Cons

- Undocumented material and geometry prevent enclosure-design comparison.
- Calibration results may partly compensate for unreported packaging effects.
- Lack of internal layout information limits reproducibility.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Use multi-site and time-separated validation when evaluating calibration.
- Fully document PM inlet, outlet, fan path, sensor placement, and enclosure material in the lab box.

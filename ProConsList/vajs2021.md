# Vajs et al. (2021): AQ10x Calibration Study

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.3390/s21103338  
**Evidence boundary:** Co-location and calibration study of one commercial AQ10x station. Enclosure material and detailed airflow geometry are not reported.

## Sensors

Alphasense CO-B4, NO2-B43F, SO2 and O3 cells; Bosch BME280; Plantower PMS7003.

### Pros

- Multi-sensor station measures gases, PM, temperature, RH, and pressure.
- BME280 measurements support environmental correction.
- Study compares linear, multiple-linear, and machine-learning corrections.
- One-minute sampling captures short-term variation.

### Cons

- Electrochemical gas sensors have temperature/RH sensitivity, cross-sensitivity, drift, and aging.
- PMS7003 requires airflow and humidity-aware calibration.
- One station limits evidence about unit-to-unit variability.
- More complex models may be difficult to transfer or interpret.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Commercial DunavNET ekoNET AQ10x outdoor station; material, internal placement, inlet/outlet, and airflow path are not reported.

### Pros

- Integrated commercial packaging supports complete multi-pollutant deployment.

### Cons

- Undocumented geometry prevents attribution of error to sensor versus enclosure.
- Internal BME280 readings may differ from true ambient conditions.
- Reproducibility is limited without material and airflow details.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Start with interpretable linear and multiple-linear calibration.
- Document gas/PM airflow and place environmental sensors where they measure the air reaching pollutant sensors.

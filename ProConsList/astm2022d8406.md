# ASTM International (2022): D8406-22 Performance Evaluation of Ambient Outdoor Air Quality Sensors

**Source:** https://store.astm.org/d8406-22.html
**Evidence boundary:** Consensus standard practice, not an experimental paper. Standardizes field performance evaluation of outdoor air-quality sensor instruments for criteria pollutants (O3, CO, NO2, SO2, PM10, PM2.5). Full text is paywalled; characterization here is from the published scope.

## Sensors

No specific models; applies to sensor-based continuous instruments for ambient outdoor criteria-pollutant measurement, portable and fixed-point.

### Pros

- Provides a formal, citable standardized practice for exactly the deployment class this study uses (outdoor fixed-point sensor instruments).
- Complements the EPA protocols with a consensus-standard pedigree (useful for reviewers outside the EPA orbit).

### Cons

- Scope is criteria air pollutants; meteorological channels are out of scope.
- Full text not yet obtained (paywalled); only scope-level claims are cited until the standard is accessed.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Not addressed; the practice evaluates instrument performance, not enclosure design.

### Pros

- Enclosure-agnostic evaluation practice; variants can be compared under it.

### Cons

- No enclosure or siting-microclimate guidance.

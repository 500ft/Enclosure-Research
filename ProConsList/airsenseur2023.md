# Schneider et al. (2023): AirSensEUR Network Deployment

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.3390/atmos14030540  
**Evidence boundary:** Co-location, distributed roadside deployment, and post-deployment evaluation of ten AirSensEUR systems. Enclosure material and detailed airflow geometry are not reported.

## Sensors

Alphasense NO2-B43F, CO-A4, NO-B4, OX-A431; ELT D-300 CO2; Alphasense OPC-N3; temperature, RH, and pressure sensors of unspecified models.

### Pros

- Broad multi-pollutant sensor suite.
- Pre- and post-deployment co-location reveals drift and failures.
- Ten boxes provide evidence about unit-to-unit differences.
- Environmental measurements support correction and diagnosis.

### Cons

- High RH affects optical PM response.
- Gas sensors require calibration and can drift.
- Some environmental sensor models are unspecified.
- Multi-sensor systems increase power, airflow, and maintenance complexity.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Complete AirSensEUR boxes mounted together for reference co-location and then distributed to roadside sites; material and internal geometry are not reported.

### Pros

- Complete boxes support repeatable network deployment.
- Grouped co-location enables direct comparison among units.

### Cons

- Undocumented material and airflow prevent enclosure selection analysis.
- Roadside exposure introduces water, dust, temperature, and pollution stresses.
- Packaging effects cannot be separated clearly from calibration effects.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Perform pre- and post-deployment co-location for drift and failure detection.
- Treat high humidity, unit-to-unit variation, and multi-sensor maintenance as primary design concerns.

# Liu et al. (2023): Naturally Ventilated Ground-Temperature Shield

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.3390/atmos14030523  
**Evidence boundary:** CFD-informed shield design followed by outdoor validation.

## Sensors

Pt100 platinum resistance thermometer.

### Pros

- Pt100 provides stable, traceable temperature sensing.
- Central probe placement supports symmetric airflow and radiation protection.

### Cons

- Pt100 measurement requires suitable readout electronics and wiring.
- Sensor/probe thermal mass can slow response.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Two silver-coated aluminum plates with black inner surfaces; white-resin annular plates, diversion pipe, and truncated-cone reflector; eight vents.

### Pros

- Silver outer finish reflects direct radiation.
- Black inner surfaces absorb reflected ground radiation.
- Eight vents and diversion pipe promote natural ventilation.
- Multilayer geometry blocks direct, reflected, and longwave radiation.

### Cons

- Aluminum oxidation can reduce reflectivity and service life.
- Complex mixed-material geometry is harder to manufacture.
- Black internal surfaces may heat if ventilation is inadequate.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Treat outer reflectivity and inner-surface finish as separate variables.
- Include vent count/area and coating weathering in the decision criteria.

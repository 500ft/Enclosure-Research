# CEN (2021): CEN/TS 17660-1 Performance Evaluation of Air Quality Sensor Systems — Gaseous Pollutants

**Source:** https://standards.cencenelec.eu/dyn/www/f?p=205:110:0::::FSP_PROJECT:65599
**Evidence boundary:** European technical specification, not an experimental paper. Defines a harmonized protocol to evaluate and classify low-cost air-quality sensor systems at fixed sites for gaseous pollutants regulated under Directive 2008/50/EC (O3, NO/NO2/NOx, CO, SO2, benzene). Full text is paywalled; characterization here is from the published scope.

## Sensors

No specific models; applies to low-cost sensor systems for regulated gaseous pollutants in ambient air.

### Pros

- Provides a performance *classification* framework (including a class for non-regulatory applications with relaxed criteria) — a useful template for this study's tiered acceptance thresholds.
- Combines laboratory and field test conditions in one harmonized protocol, the European counterpart to the EPA reports.

### Cons

- Gaseous pollutants only in Part 1 (Part 2 covers particulates); meteorological channels out of scope.
- Full text not yet obtained (paywalled); only scope-level claims are cited until the specification is accessed.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Not addressed; the specification classifies sensor-system performance, not enclosure design.

### Pros

- Classification tiers are enclosure-agnostic and can label enclosure variants by achieved data quality.

### Cons

- No enclosure or airflow guidance.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports framing acceptance thresholds as performance classes rather than a single pass/fail line.
- Cannot justify claims beyond its scope statement until the full text is obtained through library access.

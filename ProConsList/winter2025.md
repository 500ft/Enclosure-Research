# Winter et al. (2025): Sustained Electrochemical-Sensor Performance

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1021/acssensors.5c00566  
**Evidence boundary:** Three-year BEACO2N field evaluation focused on sensing-element lifetime and calibration, not enclosure construction.

## Sensors

Alphasense NO2-B43F, Ox-B431, NO-B4, and CO-B4 electrochemical sensors.

### Pros

- Calibrated measurements remained consistent over three years.
- Monthly calibration updates accounted for seasonal changes.
- Dense-network evidence supports long-term electrochemical-sensor use.

### Cons

- Raw response changes seasonally and requires frequent calibration.
- Approximately 10% of boards were switched within 18 months because of sensor malfunctions.
- Mechanical or electrical failures can occur before sensing-element degradation.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

BEACO2N nodes were used, but enclosure material and geometry are not the focus of this paper.

### Pros

- Complete network deployment reveals actual service-life limitations.

### Cons

- Enclosure contributions cannot be isolated.
- Missing packaging detail prevents material or airflow selection.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Define recalibration interval separately from physical-maintenance interval.
- Track mechanical/electrical failures rather than assuming sensor aging is the first limit.

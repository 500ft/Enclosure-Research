# Diez et al. (2024): QUANT Commercial Sensor Evaluation

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.5194/amt-17-3809-2024  
**Evidence boundary:** Three-year evaluation of 43 commercial devices containing 119 gas and 118 PM sensors across UK urban environments.

## Sensors

Multiple commercial gas and particulate-matter sensor systems from several manufacturers.

### Pros

- Large multi-device dataset exposes inter-device precision and long-term stability.
- Relocation across environments tests calibration robustness.
- Multiple metrics and uncertainty analysis improve evaluation rigor.

### Cons

- Performance varied substantially between systems.
- Manufacturer corrections can change during deployment.
- Commercial black boxes obscure sensing elements, processing, and calibration.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Commercial protective cases with sampling, power, acquisition, processing, and communications; detailed materials and internal layouts are generally proprietary.

### Pros

- Complete systems can be deployed without custom integration.
- Long-term field data capture total system performance.

### Cons

- Packaging and airflow effects cannot be separated from algorithms and sensors.
- Undocumented changes reduce reproducibility.
- Relocation can reduce accuracy despite unchanged hardware.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Document every hardware, firmware, and calibration change.
- Evaluate inter-device precision, relocation, and uncertainty in addition to RMSE.

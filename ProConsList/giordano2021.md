# Giordano et al. (2021): Low-Cost PM Calibration Review

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaerosci.2021.105833  
**Evidence boundary:** Literature review and best-practices synthesis; no single sensor box, material, or geometry.

## Sensors

Low-cost optical particulate-matter sensors as a class.

### Pros

- Reviews recurring PM calibration problems across many studies.
- Establishes humidity, aerosol properties, sensor age, and site as important variables.
- Supports reference co-location and transparent calibration evaluation.

### Cons

- No single PM model can be selected from the review alone.
- Sensor performance varies by aerosol source and deployment conditions.
- Calibration conclusions are not automatically transferable to a new site.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

No single enclosure is evaluated; packaging and airflow are identified as influences on PM performance.

### Pros

- Supports controlling and documenting inlet flow, airflow, and packaging.
- Provides a basis for testing humidity effects and sensor aging.

### Cons

- Does not rank materials or provide an enclosure geometry.
- Cannot isolate packaging effects from sensor and aerosol effects.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- PM testing must include humidity, inlet/airflow documentation, aging, and local reference co-location.
- Do not select a material or geometry based on this review alone.
